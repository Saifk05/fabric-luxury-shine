Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fabric Luxury Shine collects, uses, stores and protects information when customers interact with our laundry, fabric care, website and WhatsApp communication services.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. About Fabric Luxury Shine

Fabric Luxury Shine provides laundry, garment cleaning, fabric care, pickup, delivery and related customer support services.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website, telephone communication, WhatsApp communications and interactions related to our services.

2. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that customers provide directly to us, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile or WhatsApp number
  • Pickup and delivery details
  • Service requirements
  • Order information
  • Payment-related transaction information
  • Messages and customer support communications

3. How We Use Information

Customer information may be used to:

  • Process and manage laundry or fabric care orders
  • Schedule pickup and delivery
  • Provide order status updates
  • Send invoices, bills or receipts
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to customer enquiries
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Maintain business and transaction records

4. WhatsApp Communications

Fabric Luxury Shine may use WhatsApp Business Platform services to communicate with customers regarding their enquiries, orders, pickup, delivery, receipts, service updates and customer support.

Customer phone numbers and relevant message information may be processed through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for the purpose of delivering these communications.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, communicate with customers, process transactions or comply with applicable legal obligations.

These service providers may include communication, hosting, payment and technology providers.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no online system or electronic communication method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain transaction and business records, resolve disputes and comply with legal requirements.

8. Customer Rights

Customers may contact us regarding the personal information associated with their interactions with Fabric Luxury Shine.

Customers may also request correction or deletion of personal information where applicable, subject to legitimate business, accounting or legal requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Our services may rely on third-party platforms such as Meta and WhatsApp for customer communication.

Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Customers are encouraged to review the privacy practices of any third-party platform they use.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Fabric Luxury Shine may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to our services, technology, business practices or applicable requirements.

The latest version will always be published on this page.
