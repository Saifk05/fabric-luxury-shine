Data Deletion Instructions

Information about requesting deletion of your personal data from Fabric Luxury Shine.

Fabric Luxury Shine respects your privacy and provides customers with the ability to request deletion of personal information associated with our services.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like us to delete personal information associated with you, please contact Fabric Luxury Shine and submit a data deletion request.

When submitting your request, please provide sufficient information for us to identify the relevant customer record, such as your name and the phone number used when communicating with our business.

Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on your interactions with Fabric Luxury Shine, your request may relate to information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Phone number
  • Customer communication records
  • Laundry order information
  • Other personal information associated with our services

Processing Your Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will review the request and delete or anonymize applicable personal information where appropriate.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business record-keeping purposes.

WhatsApp Communications

Fabric Luxury Shine may use WhatsApp to provide order updates, invoices, customer support, and other service-related communications.

You may request deletion of personal information maintained by Fabric Luxury Shine in connection with these communications by contacting us.

For data deletion requests or privacy-related questions, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.
